Our Story

Kasawo's land, people and traditions, told by those who know them best.

Where we are

In the heart of Kyaggwe, Buganda

Kasawo is a town and sub-county in Mukono District, in Uganda's Central Region, about 62 km northeast of Kampala by road.

Its life follows the land: banana groves, pineapple and coffee gardens, busy market days and close family ties. This page is where Kasawo's history will be gathered from elders, families and local records.
